# Supplier trading terms (demo data)

Internal summary of the trading terms agreed with our main suppliers. Purchasing, accounts and the owner only; do not share with customers or other suppliers.

## Nestle Products Sdn Bhd

### Ordering and delivery
Minimum order is RM500 per delivery before tax. Orders placed by Tuesday 5pm are delivered on Thursday. Deliveries below the minimum are charged a RM25 delivery fee, which appears as a deduction on their payment advice.

### Payment
Payment terms are 30 days from the invoice date. We pay by bank transfer; Nestle sends a payment advice listing the invoices and credit notes it covers.

### Returns and credit notes
Expired or damaged Nestle goods can be returned within 14 days of the expiry date, with a return note signed by their salesman. Nestle issues a credit note (CN) within two weeks, quoting the original invoice number. Until the CN arrives we record a provisional supplier CN in the system.

### Rebate
A quarterly volume rebate of 2% applies when purchases in the quarter exceed RM30,000 before tax. The rebate is paid as a credit note in the following month.

## Roti Segar Sdn Bhd (bakery)

### Delivery
Bread is delivered every morning before 8am except Sunday. The driver takes back yesterday's unsold bread on the same visit.

### Unsold bread and credit notes
Unsold bread returned the next morning is credited in full. Roti Segar issues one credit note per week covering all returns of that week, usually on Monday; the CN number starts with RS-CN.

### Payment
Payment is weekly, every Friday, for the deliveries of the previous week less that week's credit note. Small differences under RM2 are written off as rounding.
